How it works

  1. Upload from your phone

    Join the kiosk Wi-Fi, open the page, and choose a PDF, photo, or Office file. The page count and price appear right away.

  2. Insert your coins

    The coin slot counts your pesos and your credit updates live on your phone. The kiosk gives no change, so pay close to the total.

  3. Collect your pages

    Press Print. You are charged only after the printer accepts the job, so a printer error costs you nothing.

Something wrong with your print?

Wrong or error print, or a paper jam? Send a photo of the problem with your GCash name and number. The owner checks it and sends your refund to GCash within 3 days.

Photos and numbers go only to the shop owner.
